## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes editorial clarity with structural mechanical engineering aesthetics. Built for high-performance industrial and engineering enterprise contexts, it rejects decorative frivolity, synthetic glassmorphism, and neon gradients in favor of absolute precision, functional density, and architectural rhythm.

The visual narrative expresses disciplined competence and quiet authority. Whitespace operates as a deliberate engineering tolerance rather than mere emptiness, creating focus and technical calm. The palette is dominated by stark architectural whites, technical grays, and structural charcoal, punctuated intentionally by calibrated telemetry crimson.

### Principles
- **Engineering Rigor**: Every element exists on a calculated structural grid. Alignments are crisp, hairlines are structural, and metadata is elevated to an informative design feature.
- **Editorial Cadence**: Generous leading, deliberate typographic contrast, and restrained scale communicate an executive, high-trust tone.
- **Calibrated Focal Points**: High-chroma crimson is conserved strictly for operational emphasis, decisive calls-to-action, and critical telemetry states.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around structural clarity and high-contrast legibility.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Primary Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure architectural white for base application surfaces, key cards, and structured layouts.
- **Sub-surface Tiers (`#F8F9FA`, `#F3F4F6`)**: Structural alternating tiers used for section backgrounds, technical panels, and neutral containers.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E4E8`, `#CBD0D8`)**: Calibrated hairlines defining perimeter bounds, column divisions, and input states.

### Ink & Telemetry
- **Primary Ink (`#111315`)**: Deep carbon black for primary headlines, critical telemetry values, and core visual anchors.
- **Secondary Ink (`#1E2024`)**: Charcoal for structured subheadings, structural lines, and active icon states.
- **Muted Steel Ink (`#59616D`)**: Technical mid-tone for body descriptions, table headers, and inactive states.
- **Precision Crimson (`#C8102E`)**: High-contrast, strictly conserved indicator for primary interactive triggers, active breadcrumbs, and real-time status pulses.

## Typography

The typographic engine balances mechanical geometry with editorial scannability.

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, structural proportions with optical balance. Used at controlled, disciplined scales to maintain architectural presence without brute visual noise.
- **Body Text (`Inter`)**: Neutral, ultra-legible grotesque set with deliberate line height ratios (1.6 to 1.7) ensuring relaxed, editorial reading rhythms across dense technical narratives.
- **Telemetry & Labels (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, monospaced tabular numbers and uppercase indicators for engineering tolerances, telemetry readouts, status pills, and system metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an editorial 12-column grid anchored by precise horizontal bands. 

### Grid Rules
- **Desktop (≥1200px)**: 12-column layout, max content width 1320px, 32px (`gutter-desktop`) column gutters, 48px (`margin-desktop`) exterior margin.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout, 24px (`gutter`) gutters, 32px (`margin-tablet`) exterior margin.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 16px (`margin`) exterior margin.

### Structural Vertical Rhythm
Sections alternate between pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and architectural secondary gray (`#F8F9FA`). Vertical sectional padding strictly follows consistent bands: `space-xl` for intra-card relationships, scaling to 80px-120px for macro section divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through disciplined structural borders and subtle ambient offsets rather than multi-layered dropshadows or blurred surfaces.

- **Boundary Standard**: Structural 1px hairlines using `#E2E4E8` define elements against `#FFFFFF` surfaces. Nested panels against `#F8F9FA` backgrounds use `#CBD0D8`.
- **Zero Heavy Diffusion**: Drop shadows are restricted to subtle architectural lift states.
- **Hover State Elevation**: Interactive cards and interactive rows elevate exactly 1px on the Y-axis accompanied by an ambient, low-opacity neutral boundary shift: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(17, 19, 21, 0.05)`.
- **Structural Separators**: Content modules are isolated via hairline borders rather than elevation stepping.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, restrained corner radius (`0.25rem` to `0.5rem`) reflecting high-precision CNC tooling and metallic machining tolerances.

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs)**: 8px border-radius (`rounded-lg` token at scale level 1) to offer tactical affordance without descending into organic or pill forms.
- **Containers & Surfaces (Cards, Modals)**: Fixed 8px corner treatment with a strict 1px inset perimeter stroke.
- **Badges & Telemetry Elements**: 4px radius (`rounded-sm`) for compact, mechanical geometry.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Absolutely prohibited for structural UI, actionable buttons, or container bounds. Small status indicators remain sharp-cornered or micro-radius badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Precision Crimson (`#C8102E`), text in `#FFFFFF`, font-weight 600, padding `10px 20px` (`py-2.5 px-5`), corner radius `8px`. Hover: `#A60D26` with transition `150ms ease-in-out`. Active: `#8E0B20`.
- **Secondary**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with structural 1px border `#E2E4E8`, text in `#111315`. Hover: border `#111315`, background `#F8F9FA`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent canvas, text in `#59616D`, hover text `#111315` and subtle tint background `#F3F4F6`.

### Navigation Header
- **Dimensions**: Strict fixed height of 76px.
- **Surface**: Solid `#FFFFFF` anchored by a subtle bottom boundary (`1px solid #E2E4E8`).
- **Hierarchy**: Left-aligned wordmark/logo in `#111315`; center/offset navigation links in `#59616D` (hover `#111315`) set in `Inter` 14px weight 500; right cluster housing direct engineering contact/telemetry and one primary crimson button.

### Badges & Technical Indicators
- **Style**: JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase with `0.05em` letter tracking. 
- **Surface**: `#F3F4F6` background with hairline stroke `#E2E4E8`.
- **Status Variants**: Precision Crimson dot (`6px x 6px`) or stroke indicator for live telemetry, operational flags, and error status.

### Data Cards & Content Containers
- **Surface**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` canvas with hairline perimeter (`border: 1px solid #E2E4E8`).
- **Layout**: Generous 32px padding, structured header zone separated by an optional hairline divider, clear hierarchy between Space Grotesk headline and Inter body copy.
- **Hover**: 1px micro-lift with border color transition to `#CBD0D8` and ambient tint shadow.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Container**: White surface with `1px solid #CBD0D8` stroke, 8px radius, 40px standard height.
- **Text**: `#111315` input copy with `#59616D` placeholder.
- **Focus**: Hairline border shifts to `#111315` accompanied by an unblurred `1px` structural ring (`outline: 1px solid #111315`). Error state engages `#C8102E` outline.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes**: 18px square, 3px corner radius, hairline stroke `#CBD0D8`. Active state filled with `#111315` or `#C8102E` with pure white precision check icon.
- **Segmented Switchers**: Surface set in `#F3F4F6` with 4px inner padding. Active toggle elevated on pure `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E4E8` and micro ambient shadow.
